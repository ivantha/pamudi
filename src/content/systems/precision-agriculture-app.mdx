argument:
  - label: The problem
    lead: Growers were reading hardware, not crops.
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        Substrate sensors, irrigation controllers and crop records each had
        their own tool and their own vocabulary. A grower deciding whether to
        irrigate a room had to hold three readings in their head and translate
        between units, in a building where they are standing up, gloved, and in
        a hurry.
      - >-
        The data was not the constraint. Density was: everything mattered,
        nothing could be hidden behind a tab, and the screen had to be legible
        at arm's length.
  - label: The approach
    lead: One room, one row, one glance.
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        The facility view reduces each room to a single line that answers the
        only question being asked on the floor: is this room within range.
        Detail lives one level down, in a room view that keeps the same reading
        order so the eye does not have to relearn the layout.
      - >-
        Status is carried by value and label as well as colour, so the system
        holds up in bright rooms and for colour-blind users. Contrast and
        content sizing were checked at token level, before any screen was drawn.
